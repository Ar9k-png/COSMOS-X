🚀 Space Missions

Humanity has always looked beyond Earth. These missions helped us understand the universe.

🌙 Apollo 11

The first successful human mission to land astronauts on the Moon. Neil Armstrong became the first person to walk on the lunar surface in 1969.

🔴 Mars Rover Missions

Robotic explorers like Curiosity and Perseverance study Mars, searching for clues about the planet's ancient history.

🛰 James Webb Telescope

The James Webb Space Telescope observes distant galaxies, stars, and planets with incredible detail.

🌌 Voyager

Voyager spacecraft are traveling beyond the Solar System, sending information from deep space.

🕳️ Black Hole Explorer

Black holes are regions of space where gravity is so powerful that nothing, not even light, can escape.

What is a Black Hole?

A black hole forms when a massive star collapses under its own gravity. The center contains a point called a singularity where our current physics cannot fully explain what happens.

🌌 Galaxy Explorer

Galaxies are enormous collections of stars, planets, gas, dust, and dark matter held together by gravity.

🌠 Milky Way

Our home galaxy contains hundreds of billions of stars, including our Sun.

🔵 Andromeda

The Andromeda Galaxy is the nearest large galaxy to the Milky Way. It is expected to collide with our galaxy in the future.

🌀 Spiral Galaxies

Spiral galaxies have beautiful arms containing young stars, gas clouds, and cosmic dust.
